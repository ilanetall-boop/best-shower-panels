The Delta Modern 14 Series Round is built around the brand's MultiChoice integrated shower diverter, which combines the functions of a separate diverter valve into one trim plate. That matters during installation because it reduces the number of holes and connections in your shower wall compared to systems that use a standalone diverter cartridge. The round raincan head mounts on a wall-fed arm rather than a ceiling drop, so you get the wide overhead spray pattern of a rain shower without opening up the ceiling or rerouting supply lines above the stall.

The set ships with everything needed for a complete swap: the raincan head itself, the shower arm, a wall-mount handheld shower for rinsing and cleaning, a wall elbow bracket, and the valves the system requires. That is a meaningfully different purchase than a showerhead alone, and it is the main reason the price sits at $649.00 rather than in the range of a single fixture. Compared with the stainless steel panel towers covered later in this review, the Delta trades LED displays and body jets for a cleaner, built-in look and a single valve to maintain going forward.

What We Liked

The biggest strength of this faucet set is the MultiChoice integrated diverter. Instead of adding a separate diverter valve body behind the wall, Delta folds that function into the main trim plate, which simplifies both the plumbing and the finished wall. For anyone already tearing into drywall for a remodel, that is a real reduction in parts and potential leak points compared to older multi-valve shower systems.

We also like that the round raincan head solves the rain shower problem without a ceiling drop. A lot of buyers want that wide, overhead spray pattern but assume it requires rerouting supply lines through the ceiling joists, which is expensive and often impossible in a second-floor bathroom. This system gets you most of that effect from a standard wall-fed arm, which is a meaningful advantage for retrofits.

Finally, the set covers every part most buyers forget to order separately. You are not buying a showerhead and then discovering you still need a diverter, an arm, and a handheld bracket separately. Delta ships the whole faucet set together, and the included valves mean a plumber can quote the job from one part number instead of a shopping list.

What Could Be Better

The price is the first thing that will give most shoppers pause. At $649.00, the Delta Modern 14 Series Round costs more than two and a half times what either ELLO&ALLO panel or the BWE panel in this comparison runs, and that is before paying a plumber for installation. If you are comparing fixtures purely on sticker price, the panel towers win easily.

It also skips the extras that panel towers now treat as standard. There is no LED temperature display and no body massage jets here, the two features that drive a lot of interest in the ELLO&ALLO and BWE products reviewed below. You are paying a premium for a cleaner, built-in look and a simpler valve, not for extra functionality.

The listing also does not publish detailed rough-in dimensions, which matters more for a plumbed system than it does for a freestanding panel. Anyone replacing an existing valve should confirm their current rough-in spacing works with this trim before ordering, rather than assuming a drop-in swap.

Who Is It For?

This faucet set fits homeowners already planning a shower valve replacement or a bathroom remodel where the plumbing is getting opened up anyway. If you want a rain shower effect without running new lines through the ceiling and you are comfortable paying for a built-in fixture over a removable panel, the Delta Modern Series Round rain shower system faucet set is a reasonable match. It is not the right call for renters, anyone on a tight budget, or buyers who specifically want body massage jets and an LED temperature readout, since none of that is part of this set.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you need to run plumbing through the ceiling to install the Delta Modern Series Round?

No. The Delta Modern Series Round mounts the raincan head on a wall-fed shower arm, so it delivers an overhead rain pattern without any ceiling plumbing work. That is the main reason this faucet set shows up in bathroom remodels where opening the ceiling is not an option.

What comes in the Delta Modern Series Round rain shower system faucet set?

The set includes the raincan shower head, a shower arm, a wall-mount handheld shower, a wall elbow, the MultiChoice integrated diverter trim, and the rough-in valves required for a full installation. You are not buying a showerhead alone; you are buying the whole faucet set.

Is the Delta Modern Series Round worth the price compared to cheaper shower panel towers?

At $649.00 it costs roughly two and a half times more than the stainless steel panel towers in this review, but it is a plumbed-in faucet system with a recognized brand warranty, not a freestanding panel. If you want built-in LED displays and body jets for less money, a panel tower is the better fit. If you want a permanent fixture tied to your shower valve, the Delta earns the premium.

Final Verdict

This Delta Modern Series Round rain shower system faucet set review comes down to a choice between two different kinds of upgrade. DELTA's Modern 14 Series Round is the pick if you are already replacing your shower valve and want a permanent, ceiling-free rain shower with a single integrated diverter. At $649.00, it is not the pick if you are shopping on budget or want the body jets and LED displays built into the ELLO&ALLO and BWE panel towers covered above, all of which cost under $260. Match the product to the job: a full valve replacement favors the Delta, while a weekend panel install favors one of its three alternatives.
