Frequently Asked Questions

Is the ELLO&ALLO LED Thermostatic Shower Panel worth the $229.97 price?

At $229.97, it costs the same as ELLO&ALLO's Stainless Steel and LED Rainfall Waterfall panels, so you are not paying a premium for the thermostatic valve and LED display. It is worth it if digital temperature feedback matters to you. If not, the non-LED Stainless Steel panel at the same price covers the basics without the display.

What is the difference between the ELLO&ALLO LED Thermostatic Shower Panel and the LED Rainfall Waterfall Shower?

The Thermostatic model focuses on temperature control with a thermostatic valve and an LED readout. The Rainfall Waterfall model swaps that valve for a combined rainfall head and waterfall spout. Both carry LED lighting and both list at $229.97, so the choice comes down to whether you want temperature feedback or a rainfall and waterfall spray combo.

Is the ROVOGO Shower Panel Tower System a better buy than the ELLO&ALLO panels?

It is about six dollars cheaper at $223.99, and it uses a tower format instead of a flat wall panel, which takes up more space in a shower stall. If your bathroom can fit a tower and the lower price matters, it is worth comparing. If you need a flat panel to fit an existing layout, the ELLO&ALLO models are the better fit.

Final Verdict

This ello&allo led thermostatic shower review comes down to one practical question: do you want a digital temperature readout built into your panel, or not? If the answer is yes, the ELLO&ALLO LED Thermostatic Shower Panel is the top pick in this comparison, since it charges you nothing extra over the brand's Stainless Steel and Rainfall Waterfall models for that feature.
