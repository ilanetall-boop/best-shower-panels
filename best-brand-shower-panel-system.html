Things to Know Before You Buy

  • Spray count varies more than the price does. The VEVOR Shower Panel System 5 lists two body massage jets at $118.90, while the VEVOR Shower Panel Tower System adds two more jets for a few dollars more, so check the jet count line before assuming a higher price means more spray.
  • Frame material is not always spelled out. Only a couple of listings state their material outright, like the ELLO&ALLO Stainless Steel Shower Panel and the Blue Ocean 52" Aluminum SPA392M. If a durable frame matters to you, look for that word in the product name or description rather than assuming.
  • "5-in-1" and similar labels describe spray modes, not jets. The HSYLYPA 5-in-1 Shower Panel Tower routes five different spray patterns through one valve, which is different from a system that runs several jets at once.
  • LED lighting is a distinguishing feature, not a standard one. The ROVOGO LED Shower Panel Tower is the only pick here built around a lit shower head, so factor that in if lighting matters more to you than jet count.
  • Price spans nearly $200 in this comparison. The seven systems we compared range from $118.90 to $319.00, and the priciest option is not automatically the best brand shower panel system for a typical bathroom, since several less expensive picks match or beat it on features.

What is the best brand shower panel system for most bathrooms?
