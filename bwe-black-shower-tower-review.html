What We Liked

The clearest strength in this BWE Black Shower Tower review is how different the BWE looks and sits on a wall compared with the flatter panels here. Its tower format extends further down the shower wall than the MENATT or HSYLYPA panels, and the matte black finish gives it a distinct look in a category where chrome and brushed stainless dominate. Owners researching black-finish fixtures consistently note that a darker finish can hide water spots and soap scum better between cleanings than polished chrome, though BWE's own listing doesn't make that specific claim. The tower format also consolidates controls the same way a panel does: one set of valves, one wall cutout, and no need to coordinate separate fixtures for rainfall and handheld spray. For a shopper who has already decided they want a black finish and a taller tower profile rather than a compact panel, the BWE delivers on that specific look even if the $299.99 price is hard to justify on function count alone.

What Could Be Better

The biggest problem with this BWE Black Shower Tower review is the price-to-information ratio. At $299.99, the BWE costs more than double what the MENATT 5-in-1, the MENATT 5-Function, and the HSYLYPA tower all charge, but its listing gives you less to go on. Both MENATT panels specify five functions and built-in LED lighting; the BWE's listing says multifunction without a number, and nothing about lighting at all. We compared published specs rather than running water through any of these fixtures, and based on that comparison alone, we can't point to a feature on the BWE that explains why it costs $160 more than panels with a clearer function list. Reviewers researching shower towers in this price range report that a taller format sometimes means a more involved install, since more vertical wall space has to line up with existing plumbing, so budget extra time or a plumber's help if you choose the tower format over a compact panel.
