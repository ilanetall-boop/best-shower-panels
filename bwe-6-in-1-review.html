BWE built the 6 in 1 Shower for buyers who want a full panel tower with lighting and temperature tracking in one install, rather than piecing those features together from separate fixtures. The stainless steel body houses six distinct outlets: a rain shower head, a waterfall spout, a large body massage nozzle, a small body massage nozzle, a bathtub nozzle, and three handheld nozzle settings, all switchable through controls on the panel face.

The LED lighting sets this panel apart from the AlenArt, Delta, and FUZ options in this comparison, since BWE lets you toggle between blue and white rather than relying on a single fixed color. A temperature display reads out water heat on the panel itself, a detail that matters if your bathroom's plumbing runs hot or cold depending on the time of day. At $229.99, though, you're paying close to double what the AlenArt Shower Panel Tower System or FUZ Shower Panel Tower System cost for panels that cover similar core functions without the adjustable lighting.

What We Liked

The strongest case for the BWE 6 in 1 Shower is how much it packs into a single stainless steel panel. Six separate outlets, including the rain shower head and the three handheld nozzle settings, give you more ways to adjust your shower than any single fixture could on its own, and BWE markets the stainless build as rust-resistant and heat-resistant for long-term use in a wet bathroom.

The adjustable LED lighting is the panel's clearest advantage over the competition. The AlenArt panel includes LED lighting too, but it runs on power generated by water flow rather than letting you switch colors, and neither the Delta PivotPro nor the FUZ Tower description mentions a color-adjustable light at all. If ambient lighting matters to your bathroom setup, BWE is the only panel here that gives you a choice between blue and white.

We also like that the temperature display sits directly on the panel face instead of requiring a separate thermometer or guesswork at the tap. FUZ's Tower System includes a similar display that tracks shower time alongside temperature, but BWE's panel keeps the readout focused on water heat alone.

Who Is It For?

The BWE 6 in 1 Shower fits buyers who specifically want adjustable LED lighting and an on-panel temperature readout and are willing to pay a premium for both features together. It also suits anyone replacing an aging shower setup who wants every function, rain shower, waterfall, massage jets, and a bathtub spout, built into a single stainless steel tower instead of installed piece by piece.

If the lighting and temperature display aren't priorities, the AlenArt Shower Panel Tower System delivers a similar six-function layout for about half the price, and the FUZ Shower Panel Tower System adds its own temperature and timer display for even less. Buyers on a tighter renovation budget, or anyone who wants reliable water pressure without the extra electronics, will likely find more value in one of those three alternatives.
