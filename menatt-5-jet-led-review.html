What Could Be Better

Owners put installation at the top of their complaint list for both MENATT panels. Several reviewers describe needing extra plumber's tape and more time than a standard showerhead swap, and a few report leaks at the connection until they re-seated the fittings. If your bathroom does not already have exposed plumbing behind the wall, budget for professional help rather than a weekend DIY job.

Buyer feedback also says the five jet settings overlap in feel, with some owners describing two or three of the modes as similar rather than five distinct experiences. That is a common complaint across multi-jet panels in this price range, not unique to MENATT, but it is worth knowing before you pay for five settings and use two of them regularly.

At $125.99, the MENATT panel also costs roughly five times what the FASDUNT or JDO handhelds run, and owners who wanted stronger pressure above anything else sometimes report feeling like they paid for features they rarely use. If lighting and multiple jets do not matter to you, that price gap is worth weighing before you buy.

Who Is It For?

It works best for a household that already runs standard US plumbing behind the shower wall and wants LED lighting as part of the experience, beyond just extra water pressure.

Skip it if you rent and cannot modify fixtures, or if your main complaint with your current shower is weak pressure rather than a lack of features. In that case, the FASDUNT or JDO handheld units below solve the pressure problem directly and install in minutes.
