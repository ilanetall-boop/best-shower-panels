Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a shower wall system?

A shower wall system is a wall-mounted panel that replaces a single shower head with a tower combining a main spray, adjustable body massage jets, and often a handheld wand. It installs over your existing hot and cold lines, so it does not require new plumbing rough-in, and the jet count and features vary widely by model, from two jets on an entry-level panel to eight jets plus LED lighting on the higher-end towers in this lineup.

How many body jets do I actually need?

For a single shower user, two to four jets, like the VEVOR System 5 or the four-jet Shower Panel Tower System, cover most of what a wall-mounted upgrade adds over a standard head. If you shower with a partner or want a fuller massage-style spread, the five- and eight-jet panels in this roundup add coverage, but they also ask more of your home's water pressure, so weak supply lines will blunt the benefit of the extra jets.

Can I install a shower wall system myself?

In most cases, yes. These panels mount over the hot and cold lines already behind your shower wall, so you are not rerouting plumbing the way a full remodel would require. The parts to get right are secure wall anchoring, since a tower is heavier than a standard shower head, and a tight seal on the water connections so the diverter does not leak. If your existing lines are in an unusual position, have a plumber check the fit before you mount it.
