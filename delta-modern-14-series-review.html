What We Liked

The strongest case for this Delta Modern 14 Series review's pick is the integrated diverter trim. Instead of installing a separate diverter valve to switch between the rain head and the handheld wand, the Modern 14 Series Square routes both through one trim plate, which means less hardware behind your wall and one fewer part that can fail over time. Delta also includes every piece needed for the swap, the shower arm, the wall elbow, the handheld wand, and the rough-in valve, so you aren't sourcing a matching finish from three different manufacturers.

Build quality on paper matches the price. The Spotshield Stainless finish resists water spotting better than a standard chrome trim, and a 10 inch rain head covers your shoulders without the ceiling-mounted plumbing that larger rain heads often require. For anyone already planning a valve replacement, bundling that work with the trim kit saves a second plumbing visit down the road.
