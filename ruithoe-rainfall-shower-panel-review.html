Frequently Asked Questions

Is the RUITHOE Rainfall Shower Panel easy to install?

Most 6-in-1 shower panels, including RUITHOE's, mount to your existing shower valve and water lines, so installation follows the same basic steps as swapping out a standard showerhead and valve trim. You'll still want some plumbing experience or a professional for the wall anchoring and water line connections.

Does the RUITHOE panel include a digital display like the VEVOR or FUZMONOERE?

No. RUITHOE uses standard control knobs for each of its six functions instead of a digital temperature screen. If a display matters to you, the VEVOR and FUZMONOERE panels covered in this review both include one.

Is stainless steel better than brass for a shower panel?

Stainless steel resists rust and corrosion on its own, while brass panels like the FUZMONOERE rely on a protective oil-rubbed coating that can wear down after years of steam and moisture exposure. For a bathroom used daily, stainless steel is generally the lower-maintenance material.
